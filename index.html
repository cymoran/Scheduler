How to use

Hover over any 30-minute calendar segment and right-click to add a client session, blocked time, or custom item.

Drag a client card over the calendar. The preview snaps to each 15-minute mark, similar to a modern calendar application.

Move scheduled blocks by dragging them. Resize a block with the handle along its bottom edge.

For blocked time, drag the lower-right corner down to add time or sideways to cover more weekdays.
